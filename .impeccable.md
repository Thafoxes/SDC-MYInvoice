## Design Context

### Users
Small-business sellers reviewing invoices and payment follow-ups in NudgeWise.

### Brand Personality
Simple, clear, familiar. Keep the existing product styling.

### Aesthetic Direction
The user explicitly chose: "Keep the existing visual style; simplify the layout only."
Preserve the light blue palette, typography, rounded surfaces, and existing components.

### Design Principles
- Put invoice review and follow-up work before supporting reports.
- Reveal customer evidence and charts on demand.
- Keep financial safeguards and demo disclosures visible where relevant.
- Reuse existing components; avoid extra dependencies for basic layout work.
- Keep controls accessible by keyboard and usable on small screens.
